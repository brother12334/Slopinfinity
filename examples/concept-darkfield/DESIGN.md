# Darkfield Astronomy Club (concept)

**Brief (fictional, written for this test):** free observing sessions on the Saturday
nearest each new moon, at the Ridgeway car park, starting one hour after sunset. Cancelled
if the sky is cloudy at the start time. Members bring and share telescopes. Bring warm
clothes and a red torch. Contact hello@example.org. Primary action: come to a session.

**Idea:** an almanac. The hero is a December 2026 calendar showing the Moon as it looks
each evening, with the session night marked, so the page shows when the club meets and
why. A strip shows one full lunar month below it.

**Data:** new-moon times from Meeus, *Astronomical Algorithms*, ch. 49 (`tools/moons.py`),
checked against known new moons (11 Jan 2024, 29 Jan 2025, within about two minutes; the
2 Aug 2027 new moon matches that day's total solar eclipse). Phases are for 21:00 UTC.
Session dates are worked out from UTC; a club far from UTC could see a date shift.

**Moon drawing:** the near side's main maria and Tycho in approximate positions, drawn in
SVG. A licensed Moon photograph was tried once on Wikimedia Commons and was rate-limited.

**Verdict:** the most specific identity of the three; the main visual is real data and
answers the visitor's question. Weaker: the desktop calendar's day numbers render small,
the drawn Moon is stylised next to what a photograph would give, and the lower half is a
plain two-column list.
