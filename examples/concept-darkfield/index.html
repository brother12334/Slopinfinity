Your first night

Free
Sessions cost nothing.
No telescope needed
Members bring telescopes and share them.
Warm clothes and a red torch
White light spoils everyone's night vision, so use a red torch.
Starts one hour after sunset
At the Ridgeway car park.
Cloudy means cancelled
If the sky is cloudy at the start time, the session is cancelled.

Darkfield Astronomy Club is a fictional club made for a design concept.

The Moon drawings show the near side's main dark plains in approximate positions. Phases are calculated for 21:00 UTC each evening, and new-moon times follow Meeus, Astronomical Algorithms, chapter 49.
