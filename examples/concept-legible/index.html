    What the numbers mean

    Contrast ratio compares how light two colours are, from 1:1 (identical) to 21:1 (black on white). The Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) set minimum ratios so text stays readable for people with low vision.

    Large text means at least 24px, or at least 18.66px when bold.
