# Overprint Studio: 2027 risograph calendar (concept)

**Brief (fictional, written for this test):** a 2027 wall calendar, 12 months and a cover,
A3, risograph printed in fluorescent pink and blue on off-white paper. Edition of 200,
$38. Orders by email to orders@example.com. Ships December 2026. Primary action: order.

**Idea:** the product is a printed design, so the page shows the actual page designs
(`tools/sheets.py`): a blue numeral and a pink shape per month, overprinted with multiply
so overlaps go purple, with ink grain and slight misregistration, and the real 2027 date
grid. The site uses the same two inks and paper colour.

**Labelled:** "The page designs. Photos of the printed calendar aren't available yet."
The studio and email address are marked fictional in the footer.

**Verdict:** the most visually striking of the three, and honest because the designs are
the product. Weaker: numeral-plus-shape riso posters are a familiar genre, so the look is
well made rather than new; the spec list appears twice (hero and details); on mobile the
fanned sheets in the hero are small.

**Unresolved:** photos of printed copies, paper weight, payment method, shipping costs
and countries.
