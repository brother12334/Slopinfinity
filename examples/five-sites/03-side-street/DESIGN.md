# Side Street

- **Visitor's job:** find a story to read.
- **Content used:** three lead headlines. No article text, authors or city name (none supplied;
  the city stays unnamed). Section labels "Places" and "People" are editorial grouping only.
- **Real sites studied:** The Pudding (each story gets a visual decided by its subject, with a
  shared, navigable structure), ProPublica (assertive masthead, the lead story leads), The
  Creative Independent (the index has personality).
- **Concept:** a street sign as the masthead, over a newsprint front page. Lead story large
  with its own graphic; the two others stacked beside it; a plain numbered index below.
- **Visual material:** original story graphics drawn for this page, each from its subject:
  a cinema marquee, night-market stalls under string lights, planters above railway tracks.
  Licensed Commons photos were planned but Wikimedia was rate-limiting this machine, so no
  photos were used.
- **Type (chosen last):** Overpass (derived from US highway-sign lettering) for the sign,
  labels and index numbers; Newsreader for headlines.
