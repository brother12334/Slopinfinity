Three questions a handoff answers, in the order you need them

Waiting for your review

Work someone has finished and can't move until you look at it. Approving it hands it straight to the next owner, so nobody has to ask whose turn it is.

Blocked by someone else

Work that can't start until another person finishes theirs. The blocker is named on the task, so you know who to ask instead of guessing.

Next owner

Every task shows who picks it up next and what they'll do with it. When a handoff happens, the next owner is already decided.

Handoff is a fictional product. The board uses sample tasks and people; nothing on this page is connected to a real account.
