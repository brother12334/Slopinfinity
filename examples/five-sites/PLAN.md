# Five sites: plan before type

Each site is planned from its visitor's job: content first, then composition, then
visual material. Fonts come last. Hours are shown as stated, with no live status (no
time zone is given). Photos are decided per site.

| | Juniper Counter | Kite Objects | Side Street | North Library | Handoff |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Visitor's job | Decide whether to visit today | Explore the objects, choose one | Find a story to read | Find a suitable room, understand how to request it | Understand how the tool helps a handoff |
| Content | Offer (3 items), hours by day, address | 3 objects, prices | 3 lead headlines; no text or authors | Hours; sample rooms; request form | Claim; representative interface; 3 states (waiting for review, blocked, next owner) |
| Composition | Photo-led split: food photo beside a week-at-a-glance strip so "is today Wed–Sun?" is answered without a clock | An interactive desk: the objects laid out together at relative scale; choosing one opens its detail and price | Magazine front: lead story large with photo, two stories stacked beside it, then a plain index of all stories | Task-first, no hero: hours line, then a room finder (filters, list), then the request form beside its rules | Product page: short claim, then a live demo board you can operate (hand a task to its next owner), with the three states annotated on the board itself |
| Visual material | **Licensed photo** (cardamom buns), labeled illustrative | **Drawn demo product visuals**, labeled; no photos, because a photo of a real tray would imply it is the product | **Licensed photos**, one per story, labeled illustrative and not from the stories | **None.** The task is the page (GOV.UK principle) | **The interface itself**, built in HTML, with demo data labeled |
| Primary action | (no transaction) hours + address; copy address | Choose an object; checkout shown as not connected | Open a story; article text shown as not supplied | Request a room; shown as not connected | Try the handoff in the demo; sign-up shown as not connected |
| Real sites studied | La Cabra, Ottolenghi, NYC311 | Areaware, teenage engineering, Rapha | The Creative Independent, ProPublica, The Pudding | GOV.UK, NYC311, Stripe Docs | Linear, Stripe Docs, Duolingo |
| Density and tone | Warm, loose, few words | Calm catalog, generous space around objects | Dense editorial, strong headlines | Dense, plain, utilitarian | Interface-dense, precise |
| Color logic | Egg-yolk yellow, juniper-berry blue, cardamom green | Neutral desk surface; color only from the materials (walnut, glaze, brass) | Street-sign green masthead, black and white newsprint | Civic blue on white, status colors for availability | Neutral UI with three state colors (review, blocked, owner) |
| Type (chosen last) | Bricolage Grotesque + Nunito Sans | Syne + Hanken Grotesk | Overpass (street-sign lettering) + Newsreader | Public Sans | Schibsted Grotesk + JetBrains Mono |
