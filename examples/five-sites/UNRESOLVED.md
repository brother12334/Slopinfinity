# Unresolved asset and content needs

For each site: the visitor's decision, what the current visual material actually shows,
what remains unknown, and what the owner would need to supply. Drawings and sample content
on these pages stand in for that material; they do not replace it.

## Juniper Counter

- **Decision:** is the food worth a visit today?
- **What the visuals show:** the counter scene and menu tiles identify what is served:
  espresso, cardamom buns and egg sandwiches.
- **What remains unknown:** portion size, quality and taste. The drawings cannot establish
  any of them, and the page does not describe them as evidence of food quality.
- **Needed:** photos of the real food (and ideally the room); prices if they should be shown.

## Kite Objects

- **Decision:** which object to choose, and whether it is worth the price.
- **What the visuals show:** which three objects are for sale (a walnut tray, a ceramic pen
  cup, a brass bookmark) and roughly what kind of object each is, from a top or flat view
  and a side or in-use view.
- **What remains unknown:** size, finish, color, construction and weight. Close-up views
  and captions that implied finish or construction (grain, glaze, a bookmark hole) were
  removed because none of that was in the brief.
- **Needed:** product photos from several angles, dimensions, materials and finish details.

## Side Street

- **Decision:** is there something here worth reading?
- **What the visuals show:** a readable sample story shows how reading on the site works;
  the illustrations identify each story's subject.
- **What remains unknown:** the magazine's real journalism. The sample story, its cinema,
  people and quotes are fictional and labeled as such.
- **Needed:** real story text, authors, and photos or art for the three stories.

## North Library

- **Decision:** which room fits, and how to request it.
- **What the page shows:** how finding a room and checking a request would work, with
  instructions that match the demo's check-only action.
- **What remains unknown:** the real rooms, their features and accessibility, whether a
  room is free, and how the library handles requests.
- **Needed:** the real room list and the library's request process.

## Handoff

- **Decision:** would this tool help my team hand work over?
- **What the visuals show:** the demo demonstrates the handoff mechanism: who is waiting on
  whom, who has a task now, and that approving passes it to the named next owner.
- **What remains unknown:** results for real teams. The demo uses sample tasks and people
  and does not prove that any team works faster or better with it.
- **Needed (optional):** real product screenshots, and evidence from real use if the owner
  wants to make claims about results.
