Schools should start later for teenagers because, research shows that teens have shifted circadian rhythms meaning that they cannot naturally go to bed earlier than 11pm. Starting school early at 7.30 am causes many students to not get enough sleep. This will affect students concentration, mood and overall grades. Districts who have started their schools later at 8.30am have seen an increase in attendance, grades and fewer crashes by teen drivers. Some people say that starting later would cause issues with transport and after school activities. However, despite this being true, students would benefit from getting more sleep and starting later would allow schools to cater for the needs of adolescents. Overall communities would be investing into the wellbeing and success of the next generation.
