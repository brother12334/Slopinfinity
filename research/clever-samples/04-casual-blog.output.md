One of the most rewarding things I have learnt as an adult is cooking. When I first moved out I would often eat takeaways and ready meals which became quite costly and made me feel very lethargic. I decided to start off by learning some simple recipes like scrambled eggs, tomato pasta and a simple stir fry. As I became more confident I started to experiment with different spices and cooking methods. What surprised me the most was how much I actually enjoyed cooking. Chopping up the vegetables and flavouring the food as I cooked was really enjoyable. Not only have I saved money by cooking but it has helped me become a healthier eater. If you have been thinking about learning to cook then I would definitely recommend doing so.
