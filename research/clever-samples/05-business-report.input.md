In the third quarter, the customer support team experienced a significant increase in ticket volume, rising 34% compared to the previous quarter. This surge was primarily driven by the launch of the new billing system, which generated a high number of inquiries related to invoice formatting and payment processing. Despite the increased workload, the team maintained an average first response time of under four hours, demonstrating strong operational resilience. However, customer satisfaction scores declined slightly from 91% to 87%, indicating areas for improvement. To address these challenges, we recommend expanding the self-service knowledge base, providing additional training on billing-related issues, and temporarily reallocating two agents from the onboarding team. These measures are expected to reduce ticket volume and restore satisfaction levels by the end of the fourth quarter.
