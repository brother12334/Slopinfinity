Customer Support increased ticket volume by 34% during Q3. This increase was due to the new billing system being launched during the quarter. There were many questions about the way the invoicing was laid out and the payment process. The team still managed to keep their average first response under 4 hours. This means they had a high level of resiliency. However, customer satisfaction dropped to 87% (from 91%). We would recommend increasing the Self Service Knowledge Base, more training on the billing issues and temporarily moving 2 agents from the Onboarding team. This should decrease ticket volumes and increase satisfaction back to original levels by the end of Q4.
