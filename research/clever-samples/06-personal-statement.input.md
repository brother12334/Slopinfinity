My passion for environmental engineering began during a summer volunteering with a local watershed restoration project. Working alongside engineers and ecologists, I helped monitor water quality in a creek that had been heavily polluted by agricultural runoff. Seeing firsthand how thoughtful design solutions, such as constructed wetlands and vegetated buffer zones, could restore a damaged ecosystem inspired me to pursue this field. Throughout high school, I sought out opportunities to deepen my understanding, including taking AP Environmental Science and leading my school's sustainability club. These experiences taught me the importance of collaboration, perseverance, and creative problem-solving. I am eager to bring this dedication to your program, where I hope to develop the technical skills needed to address complex environmental challenges and make a meaningful impact in my community.
