I became interested in environmental engineering when I volunteered in the summer to help restore a local watershed. I helped monitor water quality in a creek that had been severely polluted with agricultural run-off while working alongside engineers and ecologists. Seeing the positive effects of thoughtful design, such as constructed wetlands and vegetation buffers, to restore an ecosystem inspired me to pursue this field. Through taking AP Environmental Science and running a sustainability club at my school throughout highschool I was able to learn about how important teamwork, perseverance and creative thinking is when trying to solve problems. I am excited to bring this enthusiasm into your program and gain the technical skills I need to face complex environmental issues.
