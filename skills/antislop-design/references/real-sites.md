# Real sites by the visitor's job

Source: [real-sites.pdf](real-sites.pdf), "24 real websites, 24 different design jobs",
screenshots of live public sites captured 11 Nov 2026. The PDF shows one desktop moment
per site; open the live site to see behavior and the mobile layout. Live pages change.

**Use these to study task order and composition, not to copy.** Do not reproduce a
site's brand, photographs or exact layout. Take the principle, then make an original
direction specific to the project.

Pick at least three entries before designing: the closest match to the project's type,
plus one or two from different categories whose task order or composition solves a
similar problem. Varied styles prevent drifting toward one house look.

## What the set shows

- **The same rules produce different answers.** A government portal and a concept store
  both pass the central test, and they look nothing alike. Look across types before
  choosing a style.
- **Authentic visual material does most of the work.** Most of the 24 lead with a real
  place, object, person, product screen or piece of work. Where a site has none (GOV.UK,
  Stripe Docs, RISD), type, structure and the task itself carry the page.
- **The primary task stays in sight** even on the loudest sites: dates and tickets on
  SXSW, live channels on NTS, booking on Ottolenghi, donate on charity: water.
